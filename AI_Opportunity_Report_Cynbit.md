## 2. Industry Landscape & Market Trends (2026)

### 2.1 The PropTech Revolution: From Digital Ledgers to Agentic Intelligence
The PropTech sector has transitioned through three evolutionary waves:
1. **Wave 1.0 (Digitization, 2010–2018):** Moving from paper and physical notices to spreadsheets and portals.
2. **Wave 2.0 (Cloud & Automation, 2018–2023):** Automated payment gateways, scheduled reminders, and digital lease agreements.
3. **Wave 3.0 (Predictive & Agentic Intelligence, 2024–2026):** Embedded machine learning models that forecast tenant defaults, predict equipment degradation, eliminate administrative friction via multi-lingual NLP, and dynamically optimize rental yields.

### 2.2 Critical Pain Points in the Indian & Emerging Rental Markets
In regional and metropolitan hubs like Jaipur, Delhi-NCR, Bengaluru, and Pune, property management faces distinct operational challenges:
* **Severe Cash-Flow Volatility from Late Payments:** Over 28% of residential rental payments face delays of 5 to 20 days, directly stressing individual landlords servicing active home loan EMIs.
* **Surprise Tenant Churn:** The average vacancy in urban rentals spans 45 to 75 days, leading to direct income loss, broker fees (typically 1 month's rent), and refurbishment costs.
* **Hazardous Complaint Handling Latency:** Critical emergencies (e.g., kitchen PNG gas leaks, electrical sparking, main drain blockages) arrive via unstructured channels (WhatsApp, phone calls, SMS) and get buried beneath trivial inquiries (e.g., parking disputes, rent receipts for HRA filing).
